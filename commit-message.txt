Add recurring todos (weekly/monthly)

Todos can now be marked "Wöchentlich" (weekly, same weekday/time) or
"Monatlich" (monthly, same day of month, clamped to the month's last
day where needed). There's no background job: completing a recurring
todo creates the next occurrence right then, copying title, description,
priority, tags and assignees but starting a fresh (empty) subtask
checklist. A small recurrence indicator shows on the todo row and in
the calendar chip.

Needs a new column: run supabase/migrations/0005_recurring_todos.sql
in the Supabase SQL editor.
